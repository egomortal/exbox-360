## 🎨 It looks like a real 360, everywhere
- ✅ **Era-correct screens.** All Games & Apps, My Pins, Continue Playing, the Guide, the dashboard/update pickers, Ads and Developer Diagnostics now use each era's own look (Blades, NXE, Kinect, Metro) instead of generic boxes, and they fill the screen like the 360 did.
- ✅ **Readable everywhere.** Button hints and labels adapt to light panels and to your installed themes and wallpapers, and they meet the 4.5:1 contrast standard.
- 🛠️ **Fancy panels: no more grey boxes.** Every page and pop-up is getting a fully dressed Xbox panel: gloss, rims, the era's background art showing through, an animated selection shine, and the original open and close animations.

- 🗺️ **An idle screen** like the 360's.

## 🎬 Videos and Theater
- ✅ **Ex-Box finds your videos by itself.** It scans the console's video folders and your browser's downloads folder, and has a **Refresh** button.
- ✅ **Native playback of MP4, MKV and WebM**, using hardware video decoding, up to 4K.
- 🗺️ **Online Theater Party.** Watch a video from your console together with friends, wherever they are.
  - The host streams the video and friends join over the internet.
  - Peer-to-peer first, so there's **no server bill**.
  - Encrypted end to end.
  - Your avatar comes along. Only a tiny "what I'm wearing" recipe is sent, and each console builds the avatar itself.
- 🗺️ **Play videos and music from a PC on your home network**, like the 360's media-server feature.
